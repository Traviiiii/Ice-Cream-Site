About Whimsy Scoops

Sweet things are better shared.

We make cheerful, small-batch ice cream for birthdays, beach walks and ordinary days that deserve a little sparkle.

Our story

Born from a
beautiful mess.

In 2018, best friends Maya and Elsie started churning ice cream in Maya’s tiny Brighton kitchen. They wanted to make the kind of dessert that turns an ordinary Tuesday into a tiny celebration.

Our first market stall sold out in two hours. Today, we still make every batch by hand, using local dairy, wonky fruit and flavours inspired by our favourite memories.

We spent the next year learning the craft: balancing custard, folding in roasted fruit and tasting far too many chocolate chips. The result is ice cream with a point of view — bright, generous and never too serious.

From birthday tubs to post-beach cones, Whimsy is made for the in-between moments that deserve celebrating. We work with nearby growers, independent bakers and young creatives to keep our little shop rooted in the community that gave it a home.

Whimsy is our invitation to slow down, share a spoon, and find the fun in your day.

Our promise
Local ingredients, generous portions and a warm welcome for every age.

Our community
We donate end-of-day tubs to local youth clubs and partner with independent makers.
